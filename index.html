How we work

A coach, not a parenting philosophy

Every parenting book is certain, and they contradict each other. Meanwhile it's 6 p.m., everyone's hungry, and nobody's advice covers the specific standoff in your kitchen.

Koreviaa starts from your actual evening. One change, tested for a week, adjusted if it fails. And we're clear about the boundary: this is coaching for parents, not therapy or medical care, and we'll point you toward a professional when that's what's needed.

Frequently asked

The questions we get most

Is this therapy?

No. It's practical coaching for parents on routines, boundaries and communication. It is not therapy, diagnosis or medical care, and it doesn't replace a licensed professional.

Do you work with the child?

No. We coach the parents. It's usually more effective, and it means we're never in the middle of your family.

What if something more serious is going on?

If there are signs of depression, self-harm, an eating disorder, substance problems or any safety concern, we'll tell you plainly and help you find appropriate professional support. In an emergency in the US, call 988 or 911.

Can both parents attend?

Yes, at no extra cost, and it usually works better. We can also work with separated parents in different households.

I've already tried everything.

Most clients say that. Usually several things were tried briefly and inconsistently. We do one thing, properly, for long enough to tell.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. No contract, no minimum term. Access stays open until the end of the month you already paid for.

Which part of the day is hardest?

Twenty minutes to talk it through and pick one thing to change this week. Free, and no judgment.
